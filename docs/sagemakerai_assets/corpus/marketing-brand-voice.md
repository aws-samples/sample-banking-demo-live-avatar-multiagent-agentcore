## Voice principles

1. **Composed, not casual.** We are warm but never chatty. We speak the way a trusted private banker speaks across a desk — unhurried, precise, and human.
2. **Plain over clever.** We explain money in language a client can act on. We avoid jargon; when a term is unavoidable, we define it in the same breath.
3. **Confident, never boastful.** We state what we do and what it costs. We do not use superlatives ("best," "unbeatable") or hype.
4. **Honest about limits.** We name fees, eligibility, and risk up front. Trust is the product.
5. **Steady cadence.** Short sentences. Few exclamation points. No em-dash pile-ups. Let the substance carry the message.

## Words we avoid

- "Guaranteed returns," "risk-free," "beat the market" — we never promise outcomes.
- "Hurry," "act now," "limited time" — we do not manufacture urgency.
- "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "world-class" — we show, we do not boast.
