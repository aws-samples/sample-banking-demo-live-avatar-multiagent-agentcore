To our founding investors,

We opened Trinity Reserve with a straightforward conviction: that institutional-grade advice and a genuine private-client experience should not be reserved for the few. This quarter, we made measured progress toward that standard, and we want to account for it plainly.

## The quarter in brief

Deposit balances grew to a level consistent with our first-year plan, led by High-Yield Savings and Everyday Checking. We prioritized primary relationships — households that treat Trinity Reserve as their main bank — over headline account counts. That choice is deliberate. A primary relationship compounds; a promotional balance leaves the moment the promotion does.

Our wealth arm admitted its first cohort of Private Client households. Each was paired with a dedicated relationship manager, and each portfolio was constructed from our diversified model allocations rather than assembled ad hoc. We would rather grow this book slowly and keep every promise than grow it quickly and revise our language later.

## How we think about risk

We are a newly chartered bank clearing across the Texas Stock Exchange, NYSE, Nasdaq, and several European venues. That reach is an asset and an obligation. We hold capital above the regulatory floor, not at it. We monitor for fraud and money laundering continuously, and we treat every deposit as insured money that a family is counting on.

When we are uncertain, we say so. When a market moves against a position, we explain what we knew, what we assumed, and what we would do differently. We would rather disappoint you with candor than reassure you with a number we cannot stand behind.

## What comes next

In the coming quarters we will extend our managed-portfolio lineup, deepen our retirement offering, and continue investing in the advisory technology that lets one relationship manager serve more households without diluting the relationship. We will report on each honestly — the gains and the shortfalls alike.

Thank you for your trust. We intend to earn it again every quarter.
