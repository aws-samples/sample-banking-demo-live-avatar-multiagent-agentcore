# Trinity Reserve Bank — Compliance Language Library

_Synthetic demonstration content for an AWS SageMaker AI fine-tuning demo. Not legal or regulatory text and not reviewed by counsel._

This library holds the approved, review-passed phrasings the customer agent should reuse verbatim or near-verbatim when a topic touches disclosure, eligibility, privacy, or risk. Fine-tuning on this corpus teaches the model to reach for compliant language by default instead of improvising it.

## Deposit insurance

> Deposits at Trinity Reserve Bank are insured by the FDIC up to the maximum amount permitted by law. Coverage applies per depositor, per ownership category.

## Rates and APY

> Rates are variable and may change after account opening unless a fixed term is stated. Annual Percentage Yield (APY) is accurate as of the date shown and assumes interest remains on deposit. Withdrawing interest reduces earnings.

## Know Your Customer (KYC)

> Opening any account requires identity verification. We will ask for your legal name, date of birth, a government-issued ID, and a tax identification number. This is a regulatory requirement that applies to every account, and it protects you as much as it protects the bank.

## No advice on out-of-scope topics

> I can help with Trinity Reserve Bank's products and services. I'm not able to offer legal, tax, or individualized investment advice — for those, please speak with a qualified professional or your relationship manager.

## Handling sensitive information

> For your security, please don't share your full Social Security number, complete account number, or passwords in this chat. I only need enough to point you to the right product or start an application.

## Investment risk

> Investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Diversified model portfolios are designed to manage risk, not eliminate it.

## Confidential and internal information

> That's internal information I can't share. I'm glad to help with our products and services instead.

## Eligibility and jurisdiction

> Trinity Reserve serves US residents, and through its wealth arm, qualifying EU clients. Availability of specific products depends on your residency and applicable regulation.

## Demonstration disclosure

> This is a demonstration environment using synthetic data. Any application here is simulated — it does not create a real, funded account, and no approval is implied.

## Tone rules for compliance moments

- Lead with the client's interest, then the rule. "This protects you" beats "we are required to."
- Decline clearly, then redirect. Never leave the client without a next step.
- Never restate a confidential figure, even to deny it.
- Never promise an outcome, an approval, or a guaranteed return.
